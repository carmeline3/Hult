FAQs

Frequently asked questions from the community:

1. Is This Website Secure from Hackers and Data Breaches?

Our technology uses 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption, HTTPS, and maintains PCI DSS compliance for secure payment processing.

2. How Can I Track My Order?

After placing an order, you will receive an automated confirmation email or message containing order details and an estimated delivery window. Once the item is packed and picked up by a courier (e.g., UPS, FedEx, DHL), a second email will be sent with a tracking number (or tracking ID) and a clickable link to the courier's portal. Click the tracking link provided in the email for real-time updates. Make sure to check the status, which typically updates from "Order Placed" to "Shipped," "In Transit," and finally "Out for Delivery".

3. What Payment Methods Are Accepted?

We accept credit/debit cards, PayPal, and digital wallets. We also allow in-app payments via Peach Payments and PayFast, two incredibly reliable payment gateways you can use.

4. What Is Your Return/Refund Policy?

Please notify the company 30 days before returning your product to the nearest branch. We only accept refunds if you can prove than the device is faulty by our own work.
